What does the cleat do?

Four studies. Each keeps the approved start (EST. 1857 and the gold line) and the approved end (the real walkthrough and the five words); only the cleat moment changes. Scroll through each, or press Play. Nothing here touches the production opening until one is chosen.

  1. 1 · The mooring ropeThe gold line becomes a rope and ties the cleat hitch. It pulls taut, the frame tugs like a boat docking, and the cleat lights from the tension.
  2. 2 · Cast in 1857The line heats and pours. Molten iron fills the cleat from the plinth up, glows white, cools to blackened iron, and is left backlit.
  3. 3 · The sign at nightThe line pulls into one point of light and goes out. The real sign strikes on: LLOYDS WHARF, the cleat, ESTᴰ 1857, halo on brick.
  4. 4 · Iron over the NeckingerA 3D cast-iron cleat on the quay edge. A raking light, a walk round it, then low over the hidden river: the lit cleat and its double in the water.
