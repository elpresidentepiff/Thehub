# Legal and Commercial Inputs Checklist

> **INTERNAL WORKING DOCUMENT — NOT LEGAL ADVICE.**
> A checked box means the answer has been formally approved and supplied to the implementation team, not merely discussed.

This checklist is the gate between the working drafts and public checkout. **Do not publish the drafts or take live payment until every launch-critical item has an owner, approved answer and evidence where relevant.**

## 1. Operator identity and contacts

- [ ] Full legal entity name
- [ ] Trading name
- [ ] Company number and jurisdiction
- [ ] Registered office
- [ ] Venue address and postcode
- [ ] VAT registration status and VAT number
- [ ] Customer-service email and telephone
- [ ] Complaints postal/email route and response targets
- [ ] Privacy contact and responsible role/DPO position
- [ ] ICO registration status/number confirmed
- [ ] On-site, emergency and accessibility contacts

## 2. Products and inventory

- [ ] Exact one-time products: named desks, hot desks, meeting rooms and add-ons
- [ ] Verified space names, capacities, accessibility and included facilities
- [ ] Opening hours, excluded dates and out-of-hours policy
- [ ] Guest rules per product
- [ ] Minimum/maximum duration and booking increments
- [ ] Advance-booking horizon and same-day policy
- [ ] Meeting buffers and overrun treatment
- [ ] Self-service versus enquiry-only inventory
- [ ] Private-office and event treatment
- [ ] Clear statement of what is not included

## 3. Prices, payment and tax

- [ ] Approved price for every sellable product and add-on
- [ ] VAT treatment and whether displayed totals include VAT
- [ ] Invoice/receipt responsibility and required invoice details
- [ ] Payment methods and supported currencies
- [ ] Temporary hold duration
- [ ] Deposit policy or explicit decision that none applies
- [ ] Damage/exceptional-cleaning calculation and dispute procedure
- [ ] Obvious pricing-error procedure
- [ ] Failed, delayed and duplicate payment handling
- [ ] Refund method and target processing time
- [ ] Finance/accounting retention confirmed

## 4. One-time cancellations and changes

- [ ] Free-cancellation deadline
- [ ] Late-cancellation outcome
- [ ] Same-day grace period, or decision that none applies
- [ ] No-show outcome
- [ ] Customer amendment/rebooking rules
- [ ] The Hub substitution/cancellation remedy
- [ ] Service outage remedy
- [ ] Simple customer cancellation channel
- [ ] Cancellation summary wording approved for checkout
- [ ] Solicitor analysis of Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 completed
- [ ] Written decision on whether the dated leisure-services exception applies to desks and/or meeting rooms
- [ ] If performance can begin in a cooling-off period, required express-request/acknowledgement design approved

## 5. Memberships — complete before any recurring product launches

- [ ] Membership inclusions, exclusions, credits and fair-use limits
- [ ] Price, billing period and VAT treatment
- [ ] Start date, initial term and renewal model
- [ ] Auto-renewal decision and prominent disclosure
- [ ] Cooling-off and cancellation rights
- [ ] Simple online cancellation method
- [ ] Price/terms change notice and exit right
- [ ] Payment retry, suspension and termination process
- [ ] Pause/freeze and unused-credit policy
- [ ] Renewal/reminder notice schedule and content
- [ ] Solicitor confirms current DMCCA 2024 subscription provisions and commencement status
- [ ] Membership checkout and lifecycle reviewed separately from one-time bookings

## 6. Venue and house rules

- [ ] Check-in, access credential and ID process
- [ ] Guest and minor policy
- [ ] Noise, calls, filming and privacy rules
- [ ] Food, drinks, allergens, alcohol, smoking and vaping rules
- [ ] Events, catering and external supplier approval
- [ ] Network, printing and equipment rules
- [ ] Prohibited items and conduct
- [ ] Assistance animal and pet policy
- [ ] Bicycle, scooter, delivery, mail and storage policy
- [ ] Lost-property period and disposal process
- [ ] Fire plan, evacuation information and assembly point
- [ ] Staff escalation and proportionate removal/suspension procedure
- [ ] Rules checked against lease, insurance and building management requirements

## 7. Accessibility, safety and insurance

- [ ] Accurate step-free/accessibility information
- [ ] Reasonable-adjustment request and response process
- [ ] Accessible evacuation procedure
- [ ] Current fire-risk assessment and occupancy limits
- [ ] First aid and incident-reporting process
- [ ] Public liability and other required insurance confirmed
- [ ] Accessibility/health data minimisation and lawful-basis review
- [ ] Website and booking flow accessibility testing

## 8. Privacy data audit

- [ ] Every collected field mapped to purpose and lawful basis
- [ ] Mandatory versus optional fields documented
- [ ] Special-category data identified and minimised
- [ ] Guest data source and notice process
- [ ] Data recipients and processor/controller roles confirmed
- [ ] Signed processor/data-processing agreements
- [ ] Data locations and international-transfer safeguards confirmed
- [ ] Legitimate-interests assessments completed where relied on
- [ ] Retention period/criterion approved for every record class
- [ ] Subject access, correction, deletion, objection and export procedures tested
- [ ] Account-closure process tested
- [ ] Breach-response and ICO notification process assigned
- [ ] Privacy notice checked against actual production behaviour

## 9. Cookies, analytics and marketing

- [ ] Complete cookie/local-storage/session inventory
- [ ] Strictly necessary items documented with purpose and duration
- [ ] Analytics/advertising choice made
- [ ] Non-essential technologies blocked until valid choice where required
- [ ] Cookie notice and preference controls match actual behaviour
- [ ] Marketing consent separate, unticked, specific and optional
- [ ] Consent evidence includes person, timestamp, wording/version and channel
- [ ] Working unsubscribe and suppression process
- [ ] Service messages kept distinct from marketing

## 10. Suppliers and systems

- [ ] Stripe legal entity, account, products, prices and webhook process confirmed
- [ ] Supabase project, data region, access model, backups and retention confirmed
- [ ] Web host and production domain confirmed
- [ ] Transactional email provider and authenticated sending domain confirmed
- [ ] Analytics/error-monitoring providers confirmed
- [ ] Landlord/building operator data-sharing responsibilities confirmed
- [ ] Supplier security and subprocessor lists reviewed
- [ ] International transfer mechanisms documented
- [ ] Data-processing inventory kept current

## 11. Checkout acceptance and durable records

- [ ] Final summary shows space, date/time, capacity, add-ons, total and VAT
- [ ] Cancellation deadline and no-show result shown before payment
- [ ] Terms, House Rules and Privacy Notice are downloadable/storable
- [ ] Required Terms/House Rules acceptance starts unticked
- [ ] Marketing choice is separate, optional and unticked
- [ ] Payment button clearly states obligation to pay
- [ ] Customer can correct errors before payment
- [ ] Database stores exact accepted versions, wording and timestamp
- [ ] Payment success page waits for verified server confirmation
- [ ] Durable confirmation is sent before service begins
- [ ] Confirmation contains contract information, booking reference and cancellation method
- [ ] Historical accepted document versions cannot be overwritten
- [ ] Failed/conflicting payment path provides a fair remedy

## 12. Review and publication gate

- [ ] Business owner approves every commercial placeholder
- [ ] Accountant approves VAT, invoicing and retention positions
- [ ] Building/insurance owner approves operational rules
- [ ] Data protection owner signs off data map and privacy notice
- [ ] UK solicitor reviews consumer, membership, liability, cancellation and jurisdiction provisions
- [ ] Final terms use plain, prominent and fair language
- [ ] Public pages contain no placeholder or internal warning
- [ ] Final versions receive stable version IDs and effective dates
- [ ] Checkout, confirmation email and database use the same versions
- [ ] End-to-end acceptance, cancellation, refund and privacy-right tests pass
- [ ] A controlled live booking and refund are reconciled across site, Stripe, database and email
