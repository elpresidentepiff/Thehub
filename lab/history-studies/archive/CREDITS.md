Jacob's Island and Folly Ditch were the slum built over the Neckinger's tidal ditches, demolished in the
1850s–60s; the 1813 sheet is the earliest surveyed plan of the same ground. All three are genuine period
material of the river this study follows underground — not generated or invented.
