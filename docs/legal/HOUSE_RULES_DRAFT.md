These rules help everyone use The Hub safely and considerately. They form part of a booking only when the applicable version is presented and accepted before payment. If a rule conflicts with a non-excludable legal right, that legal right takes priority.

## 1. Arrival and departure

- Use the arrival and check-in instructions in your confirmation.
- Bring `[BOOKING REFERENCE / ID REQUIREMENT, IF ANY]`.
- Use the space only during the confirmed booking period and leave promptly at the end.
- Access outside operating hours requires `[PRIOR APPROVAL / IS NOT AVAILABLE]`.
- Do not allow an unauthorised person to follow you through a controlled door or share access credentials.
- Follow reasonable staff, security and emergency instructions.

## 2. Desks, rooms and capacity

- Use the desk or room identified in the confirmation unless staff approve a change.
- One booked desk is for one person unless the product description states otherwise.
- Do not exceed a meeting room's stated capacity.
- Keep circulation routes, doors, stairs and accessible routes clear.
- Return furniture and shared equipment to its original safe arrangement.
- Leave the workspace tidy and take belongings and waste with you.

## 3. Guests

`[GUESTS PERMITTED / NOT PERMITTED FOR DESK BOOKINGS]`. Meeting-room guests are limited to the room capacity. The booking holder must ensure guests know and follow these rules and is responsible for reasonable, evidenced loss caused by their guests.

Define reception/check-in, advance guest names, ID, child and out-of-hours requirements: `[GUEST PROCEDURE]`.

## 4. Considerate shared use

- Keep calls, music and conversations at a reasonable level.
- Use `[PHONE BOOTHS / DESIGNATED AREAS]` for longer or sensitive calls where available.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against or deliberately disrupt another person.
- Do not photograph, film or record others without permission.
- Respect privacy and do not view, move or interfere with another person's work or property.
- Headphones must not prevent awareness of alarms or emergency instructions.

## 5. Meetings and events

- Meeting rooms may be used only for lawful professional activity consistent with the booked product.
- Events, public attendance, amplified sound, filming, sales activity or catering require `[ADVANCE WRITTEN APPROVAL AND LEAD TIME]`.
- The organiser is responsible for attendance, conduct, accessibility arrangements and any approved suppliers.
- Additional event, cleaning or security costs must be agreed before the event unless they arise from evidenced damage or exceptional cleaning.

## 6. Food, drink, alcohol and smoking

- Food is `[PERMITTED ONLY IN / PERMITTED EXCEPT IN / NOT PERMITTED]`.
- Alcohol is `[NOT PERMITTED / PERMITTED ONLY WITH PRIOR WRITTEN APPROVAL]`.
- Smoking and vaping are not permitted inside. Use only `[DESIGNATED EXTERNAL AREA / NO ON-SITE AREA]`.
- Tell `[CONTACT]` in advance about relevant allergens where catering is supplied, while recognising that `[ALLERGEN/CROSS-CONTAMINATION POSITION]` requires approval.

## 7. Internet, equipment and security

- Internet use must be lawful and must not harm, probe, overload or bypass security controls.
- Do not connect network, electrical or building equipment without approval.
- Protect your devices, accounts, files and confidential information; shared internet is not a substitute for your own security controls.
- Report damaged, unsafe or malfunctioning equipment promptly and stop using it.
- Printing, screens and other equipment are subject to `[INCLUDED USE / FEES / FAIR-USE LIMITS]`.

## 8. Safety and prohibited items

Do not bring weapons, illegal substances, hazardous materials, open flames, high-load electrical equipment or anything likely to cause injury, alarm, obstruction or damage. Pets are `[NOT PERMITTED EXCEPT ASSISTANCE ANIMALS / POLICY]`. Bicycles and scooters are `[STORAGE POLICY]` and must not obstruct escape routes.

Immediately report an accident, hazard, spill, security concern or suspicious item to `[ON-SITE CONTACT]`. In an emergency, follow alarms and instructions and go to `[ASSEMBLY POINT]`.

## 9. Accessibility

We welcome requests for reasonable adjustments. Contact `[ACCESSIBILITY CONTACT]` as early as possible. Publish accurate information about step-free access, lifts, doors, accessible toilets, hearing support, quiet areas and evacuation arrangements: `[ACCESSIBILITY DETAILS]`.

Assistance animals must be accommodated in accordance with applicable law and must not be treated as ordinary pets.

## 10. Property, deliveries and lost items

- Keep personal property with you; unattended storage is not included unless expressly booked.
- Deliveries and mail are `[NOT ACCEPTED / ACCEPTED UNDER THESE RULES]`.
- The venue address may not be used as a registered office or public business address without a separate written service agreement.
- Lost property is recorded and retained for `[PERIOD]`, then `[DISPOSAL/DONATION PROCESS]`, subject to legal and safety requirements.

## 11. Damage and exceptional cleaning

Report accidental damage immediately. The booking holder may be charged the reasonable, evidenced cost of repair or exceptional cleaning caused deliberately or negligently by them or their guests, excluding fair wear and tear. The Hub will explain any proposed charge and review a reasonable dispute; charges are not automatic penalties.

## 12. Breaches and access decisions

For a minor issue, staff will normally ask for it to be corrected. Serious, unsafe, unlawful or repeated conduct may lead to removal, booking termination or proportionate suspension of future access. Immediate action may be taken where necessary to protect people, property or legal compliance.

The approved customer terms determine refunds and remedies. These rules do not permit arbitrary cancellation, remove statutory rights or exclude liability that cannot lawfully be excluded.
