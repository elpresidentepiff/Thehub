## 2. Scope

This notice covers visitors to our website and venue, people who make or use bookings, guests, enquirers, members and business contacts. Employee and contractor information requires a separate notice.

## 3. Information we may collect

Only retain categories actually used at launch:

- identity and contact details, such as name, email address and telephone number;
- organisation and role, where supplied;
- account and authentication details;
- booking details, selected space, dates, times, guests and add-ons;
- payment status, Stripe customer/session/payment identifiers, refunds and limited billing details (not full card data);
- acceptance records, including the document version, wording, timestamp and booking/account identifier;
- correspondence, support requests, complaints and accessibility requests;
- access and security records, such as check-in events, access logs and `[CCTV DETAILS IF USED]`;
- technical information, such as IP address, device/browser data, security logs and essential cookies;
- analytics and cookie choices, but only if analytics is implemented;
- marketing preferences and evidence of consent or objection; and
- information supplied by a booker about guests.

Avoid collecting special-category information unless necessary. If accessibility information may reveal health data, document the Article 6 lawful basis, the separate Article 9 condition, access controls and retention period before collection.
