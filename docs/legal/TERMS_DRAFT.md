## 6. Your right to cancel and our booking policy

### 6.1 Important unresolved legal point

**Solicitor decision required before publication.** The general 14-day cancellation right for a distance service contract under the Consumer Contracts Regulations may be affected by the exception for services related to leisure activities supplied for a specific date or period. It is not settled in this draft whether a desk or meeting-room booking falls within that exception. The classification may differ between a business booking, a consumer one-time booking and a recurring membership.

Until advice is obtained, do not state that every customer has no cooling-off right and do not rely solely on the leisure-services exception. The approved terms must preserve any statutory cancellation right that applies. If performance is requested within a statutory cooling-off period, the checkout flow and durable confirmation may need an express request and legally compliant acknowledgement.
