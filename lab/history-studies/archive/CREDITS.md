Jacob's Island and Folly Ditch were the slum built over the Neckinger's tidal ditches, demolished in
the 1850s–60s, a few hundred metres from Lloyds Wharf. Chamberlain Wharf was a real Tooley Street
warehouse, and the 1861 Tooley Street fire broke out on the same stretch of riverside. The two
"today" photographs are the actual site as it stands now. All genuine material of this stretch of
river — not generated or invented.
